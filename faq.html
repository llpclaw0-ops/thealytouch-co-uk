Questions & answers

Questions we get asked a lot

Straight answers about how it works, what it costs and what is included. If yours is not here, send it through or call 07781 446239.

Booking

How a clean gets arranged

How do I arrange a clean?

Call 07781 446239 or send an enquiry through the contact page, and we will come back to you.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

We talk it through — the rooms, the tasks and roughly how long the visit will take. You then get a price for your home, agreed with you before anything is booked. Then we pick a day: weekly, fortnightly, monthly, or a single visit.

Should I send photos?

It helps. Pictures say more than a description, so the enquiry form lets you add up to six photos of the rooms or areas you would like cleaned. Please leave people, documents, and any key-safe or alarm codes out of the pictures.

How will you get into my home?

How we get access — meeting you there, a key, or another arrangement — is agreed with you beforehand.

How do I change or cancel a visit?

Visits are arranged directly with you. If you need to change or cancel one, let us know as early as you reasonably can so the time can be offered elsewhere.

Price

What it costs

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the place and how much there is to do, so we quote per home rather than publishing a single figure. Tell us what you need and you will have a price before any work starts.

What shapes the price?

Mostly how long the visit takes, which comes down to the size of the place and the number of rooms. Which tasks you pick matters too — a hoover-and-surfaces visit is quicker than one that also includes the oven and bed presentation. A first clean on a place that has not been done for a while usually takes longer than the visits after it. See how pricing works.

When is the price agreed?

Before any work starts. The size of the place, what needs doing and the time it will take are all agreed with you first, and so are the payment terms. Nothing begins until you have said yes to the figure.

What is included

The tasks you can ask for

Which tasks can I ask for?

Everyday room care — floors, work surfaces, hoovering, bathrooms, shower rooms, bed making and presentation. Extra jobs — ovens, skirtings and polishing, inside fridges and cupboards, windows, and limescale or mould attention. See the full list.

Do you do one-off jobs as well as regular visits?

Yes. Deep or spring cleans, end of tenancy and move in or move out, after builders, and holiday-let changeovers. See the one-off jobs.

Do you make beds or change the bedding?

Aly makes and presents beds using the bedding already in place. She does not strip beds or change the linen — that applies to holiday-let visits too.

Can you include ovens?

Yes. Oven cleaning is one of the tasks you can ask about.

Do you clean windows on the outside?

Windows are cleaned on the inside, and on the outside where they can be reached safely from the ground.

Can you deal with mould?

Small surface mould patches and limescale around taps and screens can be discussed. Recurring or extensive mould needs its underlying damp problem assessed; routine cleaning does not resolve that cause.

Do you clean carpets or upholstery?

No — full carpet and upholstery cleaning is not something we offer. A stain or a spill mark on a carpet or sofa is different: ask about it in your enquiry and Aly will say whether she can help.

Practical

Where and how often

Where do you cover?

We work across Guernsey. Tell us your parish when you send an enquiry and we will confirm.

How often can you come?

Weekly, fortnightly, monthly, or a one-off visit. If you need something more flexible, say so in your enquiry.

Do I need to choose a fixed package?

No. Every home is different, so the visit is built from the rooms and tasks you choose. There are no fixed bundles you have to squeeze into.

Still got a question?

Ask it in your enquiry, or give Aly a ring — she would rather answer it than have you guess.
