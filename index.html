What shapes the price

  • How long it takes. The size of the place and the number of rooms is what moves the figure most.
  • Which tasks you pick. A hoover-and-surfaces visit is quicker than one that also includes the oven and a full linen change.
  • How often we come. A first clean on a place that has not been done for a while usually takes longer than the visits after it.
  • Getting in. How we get access — meeting you there, a key, or another arrangement — is agreed with you beforehand.

The time a visit needs is estimated and agreed with you before the first clean, so the figure is never a surprise afterwards.

FAQs

Questions we get asked a lot

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of your place and which tasks you pick, so we quote per home rather than publishing a single figure. Tell us what you need and you will have a price before anything is booked. See what shapes the price.

Where do you cover?

We work across Guernsey. Tell us your parish when you send an enquiry and we will confirm.

Which tasks can I ask for?

Everyday room care — floors, work surfaces, bathrooms, shower rooms, skirtings, polishing, hoovering, bed making and linen changes. Extra jobs — ovens, inside fridges and cupboards, windows, and limescale or mould attention. See the full list.

Do you do one-off jobs as well as regular visits?

Yes. Deep or spring cleans, end of tenancy and move in or move out, after builders, and holiday-let changeovers. See the one-off jobs.

Can I ask for bedding to be changed?

Yes. Bed making, stripping bedding and changing bed linen are all part of what we cover.

Can you include ovens?

Yes. Oven cleaning is one of the tasks you can ask about.
