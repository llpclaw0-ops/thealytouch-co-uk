What shapes the price

  • How long it takes. The size of the place and the number of rooms is what moves the figure most.
  • Which tasks you pick. A hoover-and-surfaces visit is quicker than one that also includes the oven and bed presentation.
  • How often we come. A first clean on a place that has not been done for a while usually takes longer than the visits after it.
  • Getting in. How we get access — meeting you there, a key, or another arrangement — is agreed with you beforehand.

The time a visit needs is estimated and agreed with you before the first clean, so the figure is never a surprise afterwards.
