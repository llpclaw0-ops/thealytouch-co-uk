The basics of how we work together.

What is included

Cleaning work covers the tasks agreed with you before the visit, taken from the list on the services page: floors, work surfaces, bathrooms, shower rooms, skirtings, polishing, hoovering, bed making, stripping bedding, changing bed linen and ovens.

Arranging and changing a visit

Visits are arranged directly with you. If you need to change or cancel one, let us know as early as you reasonably can so the time can be offered elsewhere.

Access

We need to be able to get in at the agreed time. How that works — meeting you there, a key, or another arrangement — is agreed with you beforehand.

Payment

Price and payment are agreed with you directly before work begins.
