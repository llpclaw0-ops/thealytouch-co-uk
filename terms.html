Terms & conditions

The basics of how we work together.

What is included

Cleaning work covers the tasks agreed with you before the visit, taken from the list on the services page. That list covers everyday room care, finishing touches such as ovens, inside fridges and cupboards, windows and limescale or mould attention, and one-off jobs including deep cleans, end of tenancy, after builders and holiday-let changeovers.

Arranging and changing a visit

Visits are arranged directly with you. If you need to change or cancel one, let us know as early as you reasonably can so the time can be offered elsewhere.

Access

We need to be able to get in at the agreed time. How that works — meeting you there, a key, or another arrangement — is agreed with you beforehand.

Where we work

We clean homes across Guernsey. If you are unsure whether we reach you, ask before booking.

Price and payment

The price for a visit is quoted and agreed with you before work begins. Payment terms are agreed with you directly.
