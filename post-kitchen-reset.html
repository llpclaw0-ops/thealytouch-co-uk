Floors & work surfaces

A simple order for cleaning a kitchen

Most kitchen cleaning goes wrong for one reason: things are done in the wrong order, so the same area gets cleaned twice.

Work top down

Clear and wipe the work surfaces first. Crumbs and splashes end up on the floor, which is exactly where you want them if the floor is still to come.

Floors last

Hoover before washing a hard floor. Trying to mop over loose crumbs just moves them around.

Let it dwell. On a greasy surface, spray and wait a minute before wiping. It saves scrubbing.
