Privacy policy

This page explains what happens to the details you send us.

What we collect

If you contact us by phone, we have the number you called from and whatever you tell us. If you use the enquiry form, we receive the details you type into it.

What we use it for

We use those details to reply to your enquiry and to arrange cleaning if you go ahead. We do not sell them.

The enquiry form

The enquiry form on this site is not connected to a mail service yet, so submitting it does not currently send us anything. Please call if you need a reply.

Asking us about your details

If you want to know what we hold, or want it deleted, call 07781 446239 and ask.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page.
