## THREE THINGS TO DELETE IN FRAMER

The template ships with proof that belongs to the demo company, not to
[BUSINESS]. On a brand-new business these read as false the moment anyone
checks:

1. The "4.9 from 2,190 reviews" badge and the star rating
2. The "trusted by 520 clients" badge and the seven-brand logo strip
3. The eight demo testimonials, and the DBS-checked badge if checks aren't done

Put each one back the day it becomes true. Empty space reads as new — invented
numbers read as dishonest, and in a small community that costs more than it wins.
