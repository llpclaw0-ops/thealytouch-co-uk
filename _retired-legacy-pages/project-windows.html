Interior windows

Sills and tracks included, which is where the difference shows

Most people clean the glass and stop. The tracks and sills are where the grit collects, and they're the reason windows look tired again a week later.

We cleared the runners out first, then the frames, then the glass last so nothing ran back down onto clean surfaces.

The owner's comment afterwards was that the room felt twice the size — which is usually what happens when the light finally comes through properly.

What the job covered

  • Interior glass, streak-free
  • Frames and sills
  • Runners and tracks cleared
  • Blinds dusted
