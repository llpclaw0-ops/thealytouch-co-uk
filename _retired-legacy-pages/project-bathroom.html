Bathroom restoration

Heavy limescale, lifted without damaging the chrome

Service
Deep clean
Property
Residential
Area
St Sampson
Time on site
Half a day
Before After
❰❱

Hard water leaves a chalky film that ordinary bathroom spray stops touching after a while. This shower screen had years of it.

The trick is patience rather than stronger chemicals — a mild acidic treatment left to sit and work, repeated, rather than one aggressive go that pits the chrome and scratches the glass.

Grout was brushed through, the shower head was taken off and soaked, and the whole room was resealed with a polish so the next build-up takes longer to arrive.

What the job covered

  • Shower screen and glass
  • Taps, shower head and fittings
  • Grout and tile joints
  • Extractor and sealant
