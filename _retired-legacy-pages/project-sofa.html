Sofa and upholstery

A two-seater, years of daily use and one very relaxed dog

Service

Property

Time on site

Before After
❰❱

Fabric sofas hold more than people expect. This one had gone flat and grey in the seat cushions without the owner really noticing it happen.

We tested a hidden patch first — always, on upholstery — then treated the marked areas before the main extraction so nothing was driven deeper into the weave.

Dry to the touch in about four hours, fully back to normal by the next morning.

What the job covered

  • Seat and back cushions
  • Arms and headrest areas
  • Pet hair lifted before extraction
  • Deodorised and left to dry
