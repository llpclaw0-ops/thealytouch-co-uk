photograph. Pexels states that its photos can be downloaded and used free of
charge, including for commercial use and modification, under the
[Pexels Licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/). The matching image in each
pair retains the source scene, light, perspective, and objects. Where a
professionally suitable dirty original was unavailable, a dirt-only *before*
variant was made from the listed Pexels composition; the oven's clean-after is
also an edit of that same open-door composition. This provides an exact, safe
comparison. The published site does not present any source as customer work.
