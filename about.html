In her own words

Hello — I’m Aly

The Aly Touch is my cleaning service, and it is deliberately small. I clean homes across Guernsey: floors and work surfaces, bathrooms and shower rooms, skirtings and polishing, hoovering, beds and linen. Ovens, fridges, cupboards, windows and limescale are there when you want them, and I take on bigger one-off jobs too — deep cleans, end of tenancy, after builders and holiday changeovers.

I would rather do a short list of jobs properly than a long list in a hurry. That is why the list on this site is the whole list. If something is not on it, I will say so rather than promise it and hope.

Every visit gets talked through first. You tell me which rooms matter most and how often you would like me, and we agree what the visit involves and what it will cost before anything is booked — no packages to fit into.

If that sounds like the help you are after, give me a ring.
