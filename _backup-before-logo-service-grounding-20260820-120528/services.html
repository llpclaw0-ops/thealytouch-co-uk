What we clean, and what it costs

Regular home cleaning

Weekly or fortnightly visits from the same cleaner, working through a checklist we agree with you.

  • Kitchen surfaces, hob, sink and outside of appliances
  • Bathrooms cleaned and sanitised throughout
  • Hoovering and mopping all floors
  • Dusting, mirrors, and beds changed on request
From £00 per visit
✨

One-off deep clean

The big reset. Ideal before guests arrive, after building work, or when a house just needs it.

  • Inside the oven, fridge and kitchen cupboards
  • Limescale removal from taps, tiles and shower screens
  • Skirting boards, door frames, switches and radiators
  • Behind and underneath moveable furniture
From £00 per visit
🔑

End of tenancy

A landlord-standard clean of the whole property, timed around your move-out date.

  • Every room cleaned top to bottom, cupboards emptied and wiped
  • Oven, extractor and appliances degreased
  • Windows cleaned inside, sills and tracks included
  • Re-clean within 48 hours if your agent flags anything
From £00 per property

Offices & commercial

Early morning or evening cleans for small offices, salons, studios and shops.

  • Desks, kitchens, meeting rooms and communal areas
  • Washrooms sanitised and restocked
  • Bins emptied and waste taken out
  • Flexible schedule around your opening hours
From £00 per hour

After builders clean

  • Fabric sofas and armchairs refreshed
  • Pet and traffic marks treated before the main clean
  • Most rooms dry within a few hours
