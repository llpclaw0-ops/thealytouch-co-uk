FAQs

Questions we get asked a lot

Need more answers?
Do I need to be home while you clean?

Not at all. Most customers give us a key or a door code. However you'd prefer to do it, we'll note it on your booking.

Do you bring your own products and equipment?

Yes, everything is included in the price. If you'd rather we used your own products — or eco-friendly ones — just say so.

What if I need to cancel or move a clean?

Give us 24 hours' notice and there's no charge at all. We'll find you another slot that week wherever we can.

Are you insured?

Fully. We carry public liability insurance and every cleaner is DBS-checked and referenced before their first visit.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover and the surrounding parishes. Not sure if you're in range? Give us a ring and we'll tell you straight away.
