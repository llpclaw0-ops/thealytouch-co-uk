# The service list — single source of truth

Confirmed by Lewis on 2026-08-24. **Nothing may appear on the site or in the
questionnaire that is not on this list.** An earlier pass had to strip invented
services out of this project once already; this file exists so it cannot drift
again.

- Floors & work surfaces
- Bathrooms & shower rooms
- Beds & linen care

## Extra jobs (ask for them on any visit)
- Oven cleaning
- Inside fridges & cupboards
- Windows — inside, and reachable outside
- Limescale & mould attention
- Skirtings & polishing

## Bigger one-off jobs

- End of tenancy / move in / move out
- After builders
- Holiday lets & changeovers
